# Manager guide

A manager sees the **My team** tab. It lists every active employee whose supervisor email is yours.

## 1. Choosing the month

Select a month and year, then click **Load team**. Each row shows:

- allocated and leave hours for the month
- how many projects need review
- the status: Not submitted, Submitted, Approved or Returned

## 2. Reviewing a timesheet

Click **Review** on a row. The page shows:

- the status line, with the submission time
- the month's project table, with targets, logged hours, actual gaps and flags

Projects marked REVIEW are more than 2 h away from their target. Investigate them before deciding.

## 3. Approving or returning

Approve and Return are enabled only when the status is **Submitted**.

- **Approve**: the month is locked as approved. A PDF is saved to the archive and the staff member is notified.
- **Return for correction**: the staff member can edit and resubmit. Add a comment explaining what needs to change. Keep it specific, because it appears on their page and in the email.

You can add an optional comment for either decision.

## 4. Printing or downloading the report

Click **Print / Download PDF** under the review table. The link opens the PDF in a new tab. For approved months it is the stored report. Otherwise it is a draft.

## 5. Notifications

In **3. Email notifications** on **My month**, switch on the emails you want:

- a staff member submits a timesheet for approval
- a staff member adds leave
- a decision you made, or a holiday you need to know about

If you receive emails you do not want, switch them off there.

## 6. What a manager cannot do

- Edit staff hours or leave.
- Reset an approved month. Only an administrator can do this (see the [Admin guide](admin-guide.md)).
- Approve a month that is not submitted.
